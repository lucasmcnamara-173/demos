What a good Tahoe turnover cleaner should handle

These are the questions we ask on your behalf before making an introduction.

Same-day windows

Can they finish between a 10 AM checkout and a 4 PM check-in on busy weekends and holidays?

Storm weeks

4WD, chains and a plan for getting to your cabin when it snows during ski week.

Linens & restock

Who does the laundry, who supplies consumables, and how low stock gets reported.

Photos & damage reports

Same-day photos of damage or missing items, so you can file claims on time.

Local rules

Bear boxes, posted permit signage and occupancy notices where your town or county requires them.

Hot tubs & fireplaces

Whether hot-tub checks and ash cleanup are included or priced separately.

FAQ

Is this really free for hosts?

Yes. Cleaning companies pay a referral fee for introductions that become paying clients. That never gets added to your quote by us, and we tell every host about this arrangement up front.

Are you a cleaning company?

No. Tahoe Turnovers is a local matching service. The cleaning company you hire does the work, carries its own business license and insurance, and is responsible for its service. Ask any cleaner for proof of insurance before hiring.

Which areas do you cover?

Incline Village, Crystal Bay, Kings Beach, Tahoe Vista, Tahoe City, the West Shore, Truckee, Northstar, Olympic Valley, South Lake Tahoe, Stateline, Zephyr Cove, Reno, Sparks, Carson City and Carson Valley.

What if the match doesn't work out?

Tell us and we'll introduce you to another company with availability, at no cost.
