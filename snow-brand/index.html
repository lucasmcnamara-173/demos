Questions

Who actually clears my snow?

Established, independent, insured local snow-removal crews. We match you with one; the crew does the work under its own business, license and insurance, and its agreement is directly with you.

When do you come out?

Plans are triggered at about 4 inches of new snow, with one clearing per 24 hours during a storm and a final clean-up when it ends. Exact times depend on the storm and where you sit on the route, and we'll always tell you the plan.

What if it's a huge winter?

Your season price is fixed. It covers every qualifying storm from Nov 15 to Apr 15, with no per-storm surcharges.

How do I pay?

You pay your matched crew directly, on their terms. Tahoe Snow Concierge never takes payment from you, and this website never asks for payment details.

Can my property manager be the contact?

Yes. Updates can go to you, your manager, your cleaner or your co-host, or all of them.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation and refund terms are set in your agreement with the crew. Getting matched and quoted is free with no obligation.
